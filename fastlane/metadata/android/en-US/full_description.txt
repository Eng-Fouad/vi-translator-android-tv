VI Translator shows a game's subtitles translated into your language, live, right over the game on your TV.

It works together with VI Translator on your PC (free, from vitranslator.app). Your console plays through a capture card into the TV. The PC reads the subtitles from the capture card and translates them, and this app draws the translations over the TV picture, where they belong. Only text crosses your home network, so the translations show as fast as on the PC, and the game reaches your TV at full quality with no added delay.

• Translations over the game, on the TV itself
• Pairs with your PC with a 6-digit code: no account
• Shows on all inputs, or only on your console's HDMI input (on TVs that report which input is on screen)
• Never takes the remote: the TV and the game keep working under the translations
• Starts again by itself when the TV turns on, and finds the PC again if its address changes
• The app speaks 33 languages

What you need:
• A TV with Android TV or Google TV
• A PC running VI Translator, on the same home network
• A capture card with HDMI passthrough between the console and the TV

The text style, the translation language and what is read are all set in VI Translator on the PC. Step-by-step setup: vitranslator.app

Privacy: the app talks only to your own PC, on your home network, and collects no data.

VI Translator is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by any game publisher.
